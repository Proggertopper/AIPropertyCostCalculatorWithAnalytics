FAQ

Quick answers before you buy.

What is a credit?

Credits unlock advanced actions in your account (Full Analysis, scenarios, compare, PDF, portfolio, optimizer).

Do credits expire?

No. Credits stay in your account until used.

Are features different between packs?

No — packs only change the number of credits. All features are available to all users.

Can I use credits on any calculator?

Yes — any saved calculation in your account.

Can I start without buying?

Yes. Calculators are free, and you may have a free Full Analysis if not used yet.

Where do I see remaining credits?

On your Account page — buttons show your remaining balance before you run.
