Refund Policy

This policy explains when purchases of PropertyCost credits may be refunded and when purchases are final. It applies to one-time credit packs sold through Paddle checkout.

Effective date: February 16, 2026 Last reviewed: February 16, 2026

Policy Summary

  • Credit-pack purchases are generally final after successful delivery.
  • Duplicate charges or technical charging errors are reviewed for refund eligibility.
  • We comply with mandatory consumer rights required by applicable law.
Scope Non-Refundable Cases Eligible Cases Request Timing Chargebacks Contact

1. Scope of this Policy

This Refund Policy applies to one-time purchases of PropertyCost credit packs used for premium features (including full analysis, scenarios, compare tools, optimizer, and PDF export).

Free tools and free features are not billable and therefore do not create refund claims.

2. Non-Refundable Cases

Unless required by applicable law, purchases are non-refundable after credits are successfully delivered to your account and made available for use.

  • Changing your mind after successful delivery.
  • Not using credits after purchase.
  • Incorrect assumptions, strategy outcomes, or market performance.

3. Cases That May Be Eligible for Refund Review

  • Duplicate charges for the same intended purchase.
  • Technical billing errors where payment succeeded but credits were not delivered.
  • Unauthorized transactions confirmed by payment-provider investigation.

Refund eligibility is reviewed case-by-case based on transaction logs, account activity, and payment records.

4. Refund Request Timing

Contact us as soon as possible after the charge appears. Delayed requests may reduce our ability to verify technical issues or billing anomalies.

Include your account email, transaction date, amount, and any relevant transaction ID.

5. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

If you open a chargeback through your card issuer or payment provider, related account access or credit usage may be temporarily limited while the dispute is under review.

6. Legal Rights

This policy does not limit any mandatory consumer rights that apply under your local law.

7. Contact

Questions about refunds can be sent to yourpropertycost@gmail.com.

Before purchasing credits

  • Review pack size and expected usage in the Pricing page.
  • Use free calculators and your free first analysis before buying.
  • Contact support if billing details are unclear before checkout.
