Refund Policy

This Refund Policy explains how PropertyCost handles refunds for digital products and services purchased through our checkout.

Effective date: March 27, 2026 Last reviewed: March 27, 2026

Quick Summary

  • One-time digital purchases may be refunded within 30 days.
  • Subscription renewals can be reviewed in a limited window.
  • Approved refunds return to the original payment method.
Merchant of Record Eligibility Subscriptions Non-Refundable Cases How to Request Processing Time Chargebacks Consumer Rights Contact Policy Updates

1. Merchant of Record

Payments are processed by Bright Market, LLC d/b/a FastSpring, which acts as the Merchant of Record for checkout transactions.

Checkout-level billing terms, payment-method limits, and payment processing rules are presented during checkout by FastSpring.

2. Refund Eligibility

For one-time digital purchases, you may request a refund within 30 calendar days of the original purchase date.

Refunds may be approved when:

  • The purchased feature is materially not as described.
  • A duplicate or incorrect charge occurred.
  • A technical issue blocked reasonable use and could not be resolved in a reasonable time.

Requests submitted after the stated window are generally not eligible, except where required by applicable law.

3. Subscription Purchases

  • You can cancel subscriptions any time to stop future renewals.
  • Initial subscription charges may be reviewed for refund within 7 calendar days of purchase.
  • Renewal charges may be reviewed within 7 calendar days of renewal if no material post-renewal use occurred.

Unless required by law, partial-period or prorated refunds are not provided.

4. Non-Refundable Cases

Refunds are generally not provided for:

  • Requests submitted outside the applicable refund window.
  • Repeated abusive, fraudulent, or bad-faith refund claims.
  • Services already fully delivered (for example, completed custom consulting), unless required by law.

5. How to Request a Refund

Send your refund request to yourpropertycost@gmail.com with:

  • FastSpring Order ID.
  • Email address used at checkout.
  • Reason for the request.
  • Any relevant screenshots or technical details.

We aim to respond within 2 business days.

6. Refund Processing and Timing

If approved, refunds are issued to the original payment method through FastSpring.

Most refunds appear within 5 to 10 business days after approval, depending on your bank or payment provider.

Certain payment methods, dispute statuses, or older transactions may require manual review by FastSpring Support.

7. Chargebacks and Disputes

Before opening a chargeback, please contact us first so we can investigate and resolve the issue quickly.

Fraudulent or abusive chargeback activity may lead to suspension of account access where legally permitted.

8. Consumer Rights

This policy does not limit mandatory rights provided under applicable consumer protection laws.

9. Contact

Questions about refunds can be sent to yourpropertycost@gmail.com.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Updated versions are effective when posted, unless stated otherwise.

Before requesting a refund

  • Share your FastSpring order number to speed up verification.
  • Include screenshots or exact error text for technical issues.
  • Contact us early, within the applicable policy window.
