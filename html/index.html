FAQ

Is PropertyCost free?

Calculators are free (no signup needed). You also get 1 Full Analysis free. After that, advanced features use credits only when you run them.

What is “Full Analysis”?

It’s our verdict report: good / caution / avoid + the key reasons and the best next step to improve the deal (price, rent, vacancy, expenses, horizon, etc.).

Why trust the verdict?

The verdict is grounded in the numbers you enter and the calculated metrics (cash flow, real returns, taxes/fees, inflation impact, stress checks). You always see what drives the result.

What are scenarios?

Scenarios are a fast stress test. They automatically try realistic changes to controllable inputs and show which ones actually move the outcome.

What is “Deep dive”?

A focused breakdown of one scenario: what changed, how it affected key metrics, and the trade-offs to watch.

What is “Ask the Analyst”?

Ask questions about your deal in context (e.g., “What if vacancy is 12%?” or “Which input matters most?”) and get a direct, deal-specific answer.

Can I compare deals or summarize my portfolio?

Yes. Compare two deals of the same type to pick a winner, and run a portfolio summary to see strengths, weaknesses, and next actions across multiple deals.

Can I export a report?

Yes — export a clean PDF report to share or archive.

Is this financial advice?

No. It’s informational and depends on your inputs. Always verify assumptions and consult professionals when needed.
