Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information PropertyCost processes, why we process it, how we protect it, and what control you have over your personal data.

Effective date: February 12, 2026 Last reviewed: February 12, 2026

Privacy Summary

  • We process data to provide platform functionality.
  • We use access controls and security safeguards.
  • You can request access, correction, or deletion where permitted.
Data Collected How We Use Data Cookies Sharing Retention Security Your Rights Children Policy Changes Contact

1. Information We Collect

Depending on your usage, we may process:

  • Account details (such as email and authentication-related data).
  • User-provided inputs and saved calculation records.
  • Technical data (device/browser metadata, IP, request timestamps).
  • Operational data required for fraud prevention and service reliability.

2. How We Use Information

  • Deliver calculator functionality and account features.
  • Generate requested analyses (scenarios, compare, optimizer, portfolio).
  • Protect platform security and prevent misuse.
  • Improve service quality, reliability, and user experience.
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Cookies and Session Technologies

We use cookies or session mechanisms to maintain secure login state, preserve essential preferences, and support core platform operations.

Disabling these technologies may reduce functionality or prevent account features from working correctly.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may disclose data only when necessary to:

  • Operate services through trusted infrastructure providers.
  • Comply with legal obligations, lawful requests, or court orders.
  • Protect rights, security, and integrity of users and the platform.

We do not sell personal data as part of our standard service operations.

5. Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as necessary for service delivery, compliance, security, and legitimate operational needs, unless a longer period is required by law.

6. Security Measures

We implement administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

No system is absolutely secure, but we continuously improve controls based on risk and operational requirements.

7. Your Data Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your data where legally permitted.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.

8. Children and Minor Users

PropertyCost is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law to provide valid consent.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates become effective when posted unless otherwise stated.

Continued use after updates indicates acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: yourpropertycost@gmail.com.

Privacy-first usage tips

  • Use strong unique credentials for your account.
  • Avoid storing sensitive personal information in free-text inputs.
  • Review saved calculations and remove unused records when needed.
