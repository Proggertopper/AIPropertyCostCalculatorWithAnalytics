Privacy Policy

  • Providing access to calculators and website functionality;
  • Personalizing user experience;

3. Disclosure of Information

Cookies are used to remember preferences, analyze performance, and ensure account security. You can disable cookies in your browser, but some features may not work properly.

5. Data Storage and Security

We implement security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Data is stored on secure servers and processed according to applicable laws.

6. User Rights

  • Access your personal data;
  • Correct or update information;
  • Delete account and data (where legally permitted);
  • Withdraw consent for data processing;
  • Object to data processing for marketing.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted here with the revised effective date.

8. Contact Us
